PG Flooring Grades Guide

Classic Collection • Solid Hardwood Grades

How PG grades work: Grades define the appearance of the wood in terms of colour, not quality. PG Flooring follows industry standards but imposes higher in-house standards. Maple, Birch and Red Oak come in four grades, from lightest to darkest: Select & Better, Pacific, Instinct, Heritage. Silver Maple, White Oak and Ash come in Pacific and Heritage only. The Pacific grade is a mix of Select & Better and Instinct.

Grades by Species

MapleSelect & Better • Pacific • Instinct • Heritage

Select & Better

Maple — Select & Better
Colour (prefinished): Low variation, white to light brown.
Colour (unfinished): Low variation, light beige to medium brown.
Knots: Sound knots 1/8 in (3 mm) and less accepted.
Mineral streaks: Sound streaks less than 1/8 in x 4 in accepted.
Other: Light-coloured bird’s eye maple accepted.

Pacific (mix of Select & Better + Instinct)

Maple — Pacific
Colour: Pronounced variation, light brown to dark brown.
Knots (prefinished): All sound knots accepted. Unsound knots 3/8 in (10 mm) and less accepted, artificially minimized.
Knots (unfinished): All sound knots accepted. Unsound knots 1/8 in and less accepted.
Mineral streaks (prefinished): All sound streaks accepted. Unsound streaks under 1/8 in x 4 in accepted, artificially minimized.
Mineral streaks (unfinished): All sound streaks accepted.
Other (prefinished): Bird’s-eye maple accepted. Wormholes accepted, artificially minimized.
Other (unfinished): Bird’s-eye maple and wormholes accepted.

Instinct

Maple — Instinct
Colour: Pronounced variation, light brown to dark brown.
Knots & streaks: Same allowances as Pacific (see above).
Other: Bird’s-eye maple accepted. Wormholes accepted (artificially minimized on prefinished).

Heritage

Maple — Heritage
Colour: Pronounced variation, light brown to dark brown.
Knots: Sound and unsound knots up to 1 in (25 mm) accepted; unsound artificially minimized.
Mineral streaks: All sound and unsound streaks accepted; unsound artificially minimized.
Other: All natural and mechanical marks accepted (checks, burn marks, sticker marks) as long as board is fit for installation. Cracks and wormholes accepted, artificially minimized. Dark bird’s-eye maple accepted. Slight variations in end squareness or width may occur.

Knots: Sound and unsound up to 1 in accepted; unsound artificially minimized.
Mineral streaks: All accepted; unsound artificially minimized.
Other: All honeycombs accepted, artificially minimized. All natural/mechanical marks accepted (checks, burn/sticker marks) if board is fit for installation. Cracks and wormholes accepted, minimized. Slight squareness/width variations may occur.

Characters Lexicon

Knots: Sound knots are solid and tight; unsound knots are loose or missing wood. PG artificially minimizes (fills and tones) unsound knots on prefinished floors.
Mineral streaks: Dark lines or patches from mineral deposits in the wood. Sound streaks are solid; unsound are soft or voided.
Wormholes: Small holes from insects. Accepted in rustic grades, artificially minimized on prefinished.
Honeycomb / checks: Small separations in the grain. Accepted within size limits per grade.
Bird’s eye: Small swirled grain pattern, prized in maple. Light-coloured accepted in Select & Better; dark accepted in Heritage.
Artificially minimized: PG fills the character with colour-matched filler so it blends in rather than standing out.
